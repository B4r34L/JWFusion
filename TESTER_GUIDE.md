## Short version (paste into Play Console → Testers → Tester instructions)

## Full guide (for texting/emailing/printing to testers)

### What you'll need first

JW Fusion combines JW Library backup files. Before you open the app, make
sure you have **at least 2 backup files** saved somewhere on your phone
(Downloads, Google Drive, email, etc.).

**Don't have any backup files yet? Here's how to make one:**

1. Open the **JW Library** app (the purple one with the Bible on it).
2. Look for the menu — usually three lines, or a small cloud icon.
3. Find **"Backup and Restore"** (it may be under "Personal Study").
4. Tap **"Create a Backup."**
5. Follow the on-screen steps to save the file. It'll have a name ending
   in `.jwlibrary`.

If your wording looks a little different, that's OK — phones vary a bit.
Ask a family member if you get stuck; this part is JW Library, not JW
Fusion.

### Installing JW Fusion

1. Open the link I sent you (it'll take you to the Play Store).
2. Tap **Install**, then wait for it to finish.
3. Tap **Open**.

### Using JW Fusion — step by step

1. **Tap the big box** that says "Click to add your backup files."
2. Choose **2 or more** `.jwlibrary` files. (If you're not sure where they
   are, try "Downloads" or "Recent" in the picker that pops up.)
3. Once your files are listed, tap the big blue **"Merge"** button at the
   bottom.
4. Wait a few seconds — you'll see a spinning circle with a percentage.
5. When you see a green checkmark and **"Your merged backup is ready!,"**
   tap **"Choose where to save it."**
6. Pick any folder you'll remember (Downloads works fine) and save.

That's it — you now have one combined backup file with everything from
all the files you added.

### Double-checking it worked (optional but helpful)

1. Open **JW Library**.
2. Go to **Backup and Restore** again, but this time choose **Restore**
   (or "Import").
3. Pick the new merged file you just saved.
4. Check that your notes, highlights, and bookmarks are all still there.

### If something goes wrong

Please don't worry about "breaking" anything — JW Fusion never changes or
deletes your original backup files, only creates a new merged one. If
anything looks confusing, shows an error, or the app closes unexpectedly:

1. Take a screenshot if you can (press the power button + volume-down
   button at the same time).
2. Send it to me along with a quick note about what you were doing.

Thank you so much for helping test this — it genuinely helps!
